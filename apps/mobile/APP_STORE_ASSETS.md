## Full Description

### English

**EKDA — Africa's Premier Cross-Border Marketplace**

Shop authentic African groceries, dried produce, and agricultural commodities from verified Nigerian vendors — or import quality vehicles, electronics, and machinery to Nigeria from global vendors. All transactions protected by our revolutionary escrow system.

**🌿 For the African Diaspora:**
- Authentic Nigerian groceries, crayfish, palm oil, garri, and more
- Dried and frozen produce shipped to UK, USA, Canada, Germany
- AI-powered HS code compliance — no customs surprises
- Real-time shipment tracking from Lagos to London

**🌍 For Importers to Nigeria:**
- Quality vehicles from USA, Germany, UK, China
- Electronics: iPhones, Samsung, Laptops at competitive prices
- Industrial machinery and construction equipment
- Sea freight specialists with Apapa port expertise

**🛡️ 100% Escrow Protection:**
Every order is fully protected. Your money is held securely until your goods are confirmed delivered — no risk of losing money to scams.

**🤖 AI-Powered Compliance:**
Our AI automatically assigns the correct Harmonized System (HS) codes to all products, calculates import duties, and detects restricted air cargo items.

**💰 Smart Payment Options:**
- Paystack for Nigerian Naira payments
- Stripe for international credit/debit cards
- Crypto (USDT/USDC) for diaspora users
- Buy Now, Pay Later available

**🚀 More Features:**
- AI shopping assistant (chat-based trade advisor)
- Price trend predictions and alerts
- Loyalty rewards (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum)
- Vendor and carrier dashboards
- Carbon footprint tracking per shipment
- Learning center with trade guides

**EKDA — Trade Across Borders. Safely.**
